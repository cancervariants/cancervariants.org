title: "The MetaKB v1 search service has been retired"
permalink: /metakb-v1-retired/
---

The VICC previously hosted an Elasticsearch-backed version of MetaKB that enabled search across an aggregated collection of cancer knowledgebase records, described in the publication [A harmonized meta-knowledgebase of clinical interpretations of somatic genomic variants in cancer](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-020-0603-8).

The original [code](https://github.com/ohsu-comp-bio/g2p-aggregator) and [data](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/g2p-0.10/index.html) remain available for reproducing the retired service. [Documentation](https://docs.cancervariants.org/en/1.0/) for service usage is also accessible.

Our follow-up project, MetaKB Jr., is focused on pediatric cancer knowledge. The service is accessible [here](https://pediatric.metakb.org).
